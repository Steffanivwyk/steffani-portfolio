Case study 01 · Scale Point Marketing

Strategy Command Centre and lean data architecture

The problem was not a lack of data. Information already existed in GoHighLevel, Meta, Clarity, meetings and team tools. The problem was that it was fragmented and difficult to turn into repeatable strategic decisions.

  • Defined GoHighLevel as the source of truth for leads, bookings and CRM outcomes.
  • Kept Meta as the media delivery source and Clarity as behavioural evidence.
  • Used n8n to transport, aggregate and classify rather than creating another raw data warehouse.
  • Used Airtable for strategic memory: summaries, exceptions, tests, decisions and recurring reviews.
  • Built sync logging and visible failure states so missing data is treated as a system-health problem, not silently trusted.
Design principle: preserve specialist systems of record, automate movement and interpretation, and keep consequential actions approval-gated.

Case study 02 · n8n

Reusable strategy extraction workflow

A real multi-branch workflow reads source-system evidence and writes lean strategic summaries rather than copying entire CRM histories into Airtable.

  • 51-node workflow architecture.
  • Current and previous 30-day comparison.
  • Contacts, opportunities, conversations, appointments, Meta, confirmed bookings, email engagement and customer voice branches.
  • Upsert patterns to reduce duplicate records.
  • Retry and failure handling with sync logs.
  • AI-assisted code and JSON development with manual testing before workflows are considered reliable.

Case study 03 · Strategy to operations

Controlled handoff between strategic decisions and delivery

The bridge separates strategic reasoning from execution status so each system owns the information it is best suited to manage.

  • Strategy owns the why, hypothesis, decision and success measure.
  • Operations owns the who, when, QA, approval, launch and completion status.
  • Approved actions can route into Tasks, QA Queue or Department Handovers.
  • Idempotent logic is used so repeated runs update the same destination record instead of multiplying tasks.
  • Operational status can return to the originating strategy action without overwriting the strategic rationale.
Control model
Strategy decision→Approved action→Tasks / QA / handoff→Delivery status
